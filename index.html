No Data   Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 14, 2026

NeonLoop is a live wallpaper featuring animated glow effects with customizable grid patterns. The app transforms your home screen with dynamic visual effects inspired by neon aesthetics.

1. Information Collection and Use

NeonLoop does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. The app operates entirely locally on your device and has no internet communication capabilities.

2. Permissions

NeonLoop requests the SET_WALLPAPER permission solely to allow you to apply the animation as your live wallpaper or lock screen. This permission is used exclusively for its core functionality and grants access to no personal data.

3. Third-Party Services

We respect your privacy. NeonLoop does not contain any ads, nor does it use any third-party analytics, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools.

4. Children's Privacy

Because NeonLoop does not collect any data, it inherently does not collect any personally identifiable information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. It is completely safe for all ages.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page. You are advised to review this page periodically for any updates.
